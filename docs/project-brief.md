# Project Brief - Sibionics CGM Website

## Project Overview

A responsive single-product e-commerce website for an authorized distributor of the Sibionics Continuous Glucose Monitor (CGM).

The website will educate visitors about the product, build trust, and allow customers to purchase the Sibionics CGM online.

---

## Client

**Business Type:**
Medical supplies company

**Client Status:**
Authorized Sibionics CGM distributor

**Product:**
Sibionics Continuous Glucose Monitor (CGM)

---

## Project Goals

The main goals of the website are:

- Present the Sibionics CGM professionally.
- Explain how the product works.
- Educate potential customers about Continuous Glucose Monitoring.
- Build trust with patients and healthcare professionals.
- Allow customers to purchase the product online.
- Provide clear information about shipping, returns, and support.

---

## Target Audience

Primary customers:

- People with Type 1 diabetes.
- People with Type 2 diabetes.
- Parents of children with diabetes.
- Diabetes clinics.
- Healthcare professionals.

---

## Website Type

Single-product e-commerce website.

The website will focus on selling one product:

**Sibionics Continuous Glucose Monitor (CGM)**

---

## Website Structure

The planned pages are:

### Home
Purpose:
- Introduce the product.
- Highlight main benefits.
- Encourage visitors to learn more or purchase.

### About Us
Purpose:
- Introduce the company.
- Build credibility and trust.

### Product
Purpose:
- Explain the Sibionics CGM features.
- Show product images.
- Explain how it works.
- Present product benefits.

### FAQ
Purpose:
- Answer common customer questions.

Examples:
- How does CGM work?
- How long does the sensor last?
- Is it compatible with smartphones?
- How do customers receive support?

### Blog
Purpose:
- Share educational content.
- Improve SEO.
- Provide diabetes-related information.

### Contact
Purpose:
- Allow customers to contact the company.

---

## Design Direction

The website should communicate:

- Trust
- Reliability

- Large product visuals

## E-commerce Requirements

The website should support:

### Payment Methods

- Visa
- Mastercard
- PayPal

### Shipping

Initial shipping region:

- United Kingdom

Shipping policy:

- Standard delivery: £5
- Free shipping for orders over £100
- 30-day return policy

### Inventory

Inventory will be managed directly through the website.

No external POS or warehouse synchronization is required.

---

## Technical Requirements

The website should include:

- Responsive design for mobile, tablet, and desktop.
- SEO-friendly structure.
- Fast loading performance.
- Accessible user interface.
- Secure checkout experience.
